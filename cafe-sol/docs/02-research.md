This doc turns the three discovery interviews (Laura, Jordi, Núria) into something the team can build from. It has three parts: findings, persona and journey. Fill in the gaps, then save the finished version as cafe-sol/docs/02-research.md.
Interviews were role-played with an AI acting as the stakeholders, to practise discovery technique. Café Sol is a simulated business.
Part 1: Findings (20 min)
Each finding needs three things:
• Finding: what's true
• Evidence: who said it, plus one example (themes only, no private quotes)
• So what: what it means for the product
Example:
F1: Serious reviews arrive 1–3 weeks late. Evidence: 3/3 interviews. A hygiene review sat 9 days; an allergy review sat about 3 weeks. So what: the tool must detect urgent reviews the same day.
